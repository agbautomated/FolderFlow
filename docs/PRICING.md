## Basis for the decision

Official vendor prices checked on 19 October 2026:

| Comparator | Advertised price and scope |
| --- | --- |
| [TeraCopy](https://codesector.com/purchase) | Main purchase selector displays **US$49.95** and currently advertises a 50% coupon. The same page retains older fallback prices, so $25 is not treated as its unambiguous current standard price. |
| [FastCopy Pro](https://fastcopy.jp/pro/) | **US$48 perpetual per PC**, or **US$9.99 per year**. |

These are broader, established file-copying products. FolderFlow's smaller feature set and straightforward Copy/Mirror workflow support a modest launch price. This is a product-positioning decision, not a claim that demand or sales have been measured.

Microsoft documents a **15% commerce fee for non-game apps**. Customer prices are not net payout estimates; taxes, refunds, withholding, currency handling, and the applicable agreement affect receipts. Do not calculate expected bank proceeds simply as 85% of the listed price. [Microsoft Store revenue model](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/publish/publish-your-app/why-distribute-through-store)
